Light and playful

What ordinary errand would you turn into a tiny adventure?

Optional follow-up: What would make it worth the detour?

What is something you tried reluctantly and ended up enjoying?

Optional follow-up: What changed your mind?

If tomorrow afternoon suddenly opened up, what would you choose to do?

Optional follow-up: Would you plan it or decide as you go?

What small thing would you happily recommend to almost anyone?

Optional follow-up: How did you discover it?

Small stories

Which familiar place holds an unexpectedly good memory?

Optional follow-up: What detail do you remember most clearly?

What harmless skill took you more attempts than you expected?

Optional follow-up: What finally helped it click?

What tiny tradition would you like to keep doing?

Optional follow-up: How did it start?

What everyday thing do you appreciate more now than you used to?

Optional follow-up: Was there a moment that changed it?

Everyday preferences

What makes a weekend feel well spent to you?

Optional follow-up: What is the smallest version of that?

Which part of an ordinary day do you tend to look forward to?

Optional follow-up: What makes that part work?

What would you enjoy showing someone how to do?

Optional follow-up: What would a first attempt look like?

What is your idea of an enjoyable evening that needs very little planning?

Optional follow-up: What would you leave out to keep it simple?

Before you use the result

Are these compatibility questions?

No. They are original conversation prompts, not a psychological assessment or a way to rate someone.

Can I use them on a first or third date?

Yes, if both people enjoy prompts. The questions do not require a particular relationship stage.

Are answers stored?

There are no answer fields. No personal stories are collected by this page.
