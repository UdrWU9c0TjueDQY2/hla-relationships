Read the fictional worked example

These are invented activities and amounts, not current venue prices. Both people allow 160 minutes and up to 25 currency units each, including shared expenses. They also set a 12-unit ceiling for shared costs.

  • Stop 1: café, 35 minutes, 8 units per person, no shared charge.
  • Stop 2: small gallery, 55 minutes, 10 units per person, no shared charge.
  • One shared parking cost: 10 units for the pair. The fictional prices include all taxes and fees.

Cost: 2 × (8 + 10) + 10 = 46 units for two. If split equally, each pays 23 units. Shared costs are 10 units, below their separate 12-unit ceiling.

Time: 20 travel in + 5 arrival + 35 café + 15 between stops + 55 gallery + 5 wrap-up + 20 home = 155 minutes. That leaves 5 minutes within their 160-minute limit. A 17:00 meeting means leaving at 16:40 and returning at 19:15 in this example.

Fallback: if rain changes the walk between stops, they will check the route and opening hours at noon. If it no longer fits, they can propose a single indoor café stop rather than assume the other person wants a substitute.

Choice: person 1 says yes to the 155-minute plan. Person 2 proposes the café only and a 19:00 finish. They have not agreed the two-stop plan; they need to confirm a revised plan before treating it as settled.
