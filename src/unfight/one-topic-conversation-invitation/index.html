MAKE THE RESULT USEFUL

Keep the invitation separate from the outcome.

  • One topic: “Weekend errands” is easier to understand than “everything that is wrong.”
  • One offer: suggest a time without treating it as booked.
  • A real choice: a different time, more space or no conversation must remain possible.
