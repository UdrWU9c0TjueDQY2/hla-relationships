Four different questions people mix together.

“Should I go?” often hides several smaller questions. Separating them can make the next step less dramatic and more honest.

InterestDo I want to know one more thing?
ComfortWere my boundaries respected?
PaceWould a different format feel better?
Practical fitIs the obstacle simply time, cost or travel?

No algorithm gets the final vote.

A maybe can stay a maybe.

You can take more time. Curiosity is not a commitment, and uncertainty does not need to be dressed up as excitement.

A concern is not a low score.

If you felt pressured, afraid or unsafe, do not average that against good conversation. You do not owe another date.

Logistics are not chemistry.

A long trip or expensive plan can be changed without turning it into a judgment about the person.

No interest needs no defence.

You can decide not to continue without proving that the first date was objectively bad.

Should a checklist decide whether I go on a second date?

No. This page organizes your own observations. It does not score compatibility, predict a relationship or tell you to continue contact.

What if I felt pressured or unsafe?

Do not turn that into a score. You do not owe another date. Consider pausing contact and speaking with someone you trust.

Does this page save my answers?
