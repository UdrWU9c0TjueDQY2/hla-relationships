The short evening / 02

When should you leave for date night?

Start with the time you need to be back. Work backward to a meeting time you can actually offer.

Turn the time into an invitation

Copy this invitation and add the place you have in mind. Only the meeting and departure times are included.

Use your own travel estimates. The calculator uses one local clock and does not check traffic, venue hours, or clock changes. No addresses or names are needed.

If the start slips

Keep the return deadline and decide whether there is still enough time to meet. Download the late-start date sheet (one-page PDF).

  1. Subtract your buffer and travel home from your deadline to find the latest time to leave the venue.
  2. Count the minutes between your new meeting time and that departure. If the time is shorter than you would enjoy, agree on a shorter activity or another day.

For example, a 10:00 pm deadline with 15 minutes of buffer and 25 minutes of travel means leaving at 9:20 pm. Meeting at 8:35 pm gives you 45 minutes together. If you can only meet at or after 9:20 pm, choose another time.

What changes if you need 15 extra minutes to get home?

Watch an 18-second example from this browser calculator. The couple keeps the same 10:00 pm deadline and changes only the return buffer.

Read the worked example

With 90 minutes together, 20 minutes of travel home and a 10-minute buffer, the couple can meet at 8:00 pm and leave at 9:30 pm. Changing the buffer to 25 minutes moves the meeting to 7:45 pm and departure to 9:15 pm. The calculator puts both times in an invitation. Add the place you have in mind before you send it.
