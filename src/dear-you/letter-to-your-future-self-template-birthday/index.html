What should a letter to your future self include?

Where you are right now, one thing you are proud of, one thing you are afraid of, one small thing you want to be true by the time it opens, a question, and a kind last line. That is the six-prompt template above, and it fits on one printed page.

How long should it be?

One page by hand is about right; a thousand words is too many to read on a birthday. The prompts are short on purpose.

When should it open?

A year is the classic birthday length. Dear You seals letters for a week, a month, a year, three years or five, and the letter stays locked until its day, even from you, per the listing.

Is the template free?

Yes: the PDF above, no email required. The app is free to start too: your first daily letter, two Open When letters, one future letter waiting at a time, write-back notes, search, Face ID lock, PDF export and backup. Plus adds a fresh letter every day, the full Open When shelf, unlimited future letters and all seven Journeys.

Is anything I write uploaded?

Not by the app: no account, no ads, no analytics; letters stay on the iPhone with an optional password-protected backup you keep yourself. Notifications never show your words. Nothing on this page is stored either.

Is it a mental-health app?

No. The listing says Dear You is a reflection and writing app, not a medical or mental-health service. If the letter is hard to write for reasons bigger than a birthday, a person to talk to comes first.

Six promptsScratch each one when you reach it

Write the answer before scratching the next. The point of the foil is that you cannot plan the whole letter; you have to be where you are.

1. Right nowWhere are you sitting as you write this, and what is the one thing on your mind that you will have forgotten by next year?
2. Proud ofOne thing from this year you did properly. Not the biggest; the one you would not have managed two years ago.
3. Afraid ofName the thing you are nervous about for the coming year, in one sentence. Future you will want to know how it went.
4. One small true thingBy the next birthday, I want it to be true that ___. Make it small enough to actually happen.
5. A question for themAsk future you one question you cannot answer today. They will write back; the app seals the reply for a later you.
6. The last lineSay something kind, the way you would to a friend who is a year older and tired. Then sign it with the date.
